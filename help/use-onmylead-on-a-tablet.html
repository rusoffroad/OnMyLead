Use OnMyLead on a Tablet

On an iPad or Android tablet the map fills the screen, with the riders, the ride details and your buttons beside it.

iPhone AppLast Updated November 6, 2026

On This Page

  2. Getting Around
  3. Ride Mode on a Tablet
  4. The Ride Page
  5. Scout on a Tablet
  6. Draw a Route and Add Waypoints
  7. Find a Ride
  8. Events on a Tablet

OnMyLead works the same on a tablet as on a phone, with the same account, rides and Pro plan. A tablet just gets layouts made for the bigger screen: the map fills it, and the riders, the ride details and your buttons sit beside the map instead of on top of it.

Using OnMyLead on a tablet or the website is a Pro feature. See Free and Pro. On a Free account, a tablet or the website opens a screen with Join a Ride, Your Rides, See OnMyLead Pro and Your Account. Joining a ride, the ride page and Ride Mode always work, so you can follow the leader on any device for free.

Turn It Either Way

Hold the tablet upright or sideways; every screen works both ways. Phones stay upright.

You can also run OnMyLead next to another app (Split View or Slide Over on an iPad, split screen on Android). The layout follows the size of the window: in a narrow window you get the phone layout, and it switches back as the window grows.

Getting Around

On a tablet, Rides, Find, Groups and You run down the left edge of the screen instead of along the bottom. The blue Map Mode button sits under Find and opens your offroad map full screen.

On Rides, with the tablet sideways, Join a Ride, Create a Ride, Find a Ride, Events, Map Mode, your toolkit and help stay on the left, and your rides, group, public rides, feed and badges are beside them. Upright, it's one column. See Your Home Screen.

Ride Mode on a Tablet

The live map fills the screen.

  • Riders sit in a list on the left with how fresh each rider's location is. Tap the arrow beside a rider to get directions to them. Tap Hide, or swipe the list to the left, to see more map; tap Show Riders to bring it back. The list starts open with the tablet sideways and folded with it upright.
  • Your buttons (the route strip, quick replies and status buttons) sit in a panel on the right. Swipe it down or tap Hide to fold it, just like on a phone. Mechanical Issue and EMERGENCY always stay on screen.
  • Directions and Add Waypoint sit just above the buttons panel.
  • The ride name, your sharing timer and any announcement stay at the top of the left side.

Tap Message at the top of the buttons panel to type a message to the group. It opens as a card in the middle of the screen.

On a tablet, Ride Mode's pop-up cards (Share Your Location, EMERGENCY) and sheets close when you swipe them down, just like on a phone.

The round target button at the bottom of the map, just left of the buttons panel, centers the map on you. See Use Ride Mode.

Follow Me sits just left of that button and keeps the map moving with you as you ride. Drag the map to look around and it pauses; tap Paused to follow you again. See Follow Me on the Map.

Tap ⤢ Full Map at the top of the buttons panel to hide the riders list, the ride name and the buttons. A slim bar stays at the bottom right with the route line and EMERGENCY. See Use Ride Mode.

Controls in that bar brings everything back (or swipe the bar up). Navigate, Draw a Route, Waypoints and Find a Ride have Full Map too; on a tablet their bar sits in the bottom-left corner. See Full Map.

The Ride Page

Once you've joined a ride, the ride and its details stay on the left, and Map, Riders and Chat open on the right. You can read the chat or check the route without losing the meeting time and place.

Leave Ride is at the bottom of the left side of the ride page, and at the bottom of the Riders list in Ride Mode. See Join a Ride.

Scout on a Tablet

In Scout (Find > Places to Ride), the distance slider, the filters and the list of places sit on the left and the map fills the rest of the screen, sideways or upright. A place's page puts its details on the left and its map beside them.

Draw a Route and Add Waypoints

With the tablet sideways, the route tools and the waypoint list sit in a panel beside the map. Swipe the panel up or tap Hide to fold it. With the tablet upright, the panel floats in the bottom corner of the map; swipe it down to fold it.

A long swipe the way the panel folds (up beside the map, down when it floats at the bottom), or a swipe on the folded panel, goes straight to Full Map.

Find a Ride

Search and the list of rides sit on the left, with the map filling the rest of the screen.

Note

Many tablets have no GPS. A Wi-Fi-only tablet can usually only tell roughly where it is, so share your location from your phone on the trail and use the tablet to follow the group.

Events on a Tablet

Events shows two events per row on a tablet in portrait and three in landscape. An event's page keeps its photo, details and hosts on the left, and the agenda, group rides and who's going on the right. See Events.
