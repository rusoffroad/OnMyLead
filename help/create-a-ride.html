Create a Ride

Pick public or private, set the day, time and meeting point, choose a mapped route or freeride, and share your new ride.

iPhone AppProLast Updated November 6, 2026

On This Page

  3. Pick the Time
  4. Public Ride Options
  5. Limit Who Can Come

  9. How Many Rides You Can Create

You lead. Create a ride, tell everyone where and when to meet, and OnMyLead gives you a link, a code and a QR code to share. Public rides show up for riders nearby. Private rides are only for the people you invite.

Create Your Ride

  1. On the Rides tab, tap Create a Ride.
  2. Under What Kind of Ride?, tap Public (open to anyone, found by riders near you or in your state) or Private (only friends you invite by link, text or email).
  3. Type a Ride Name.
  4. Under When, pick the day (see Pick the Day below).
  5. Set the time in Meet At (see Pick the Time below).
  6. Set the Meeting Point. You can search in Search a Place or Address, tap Use My Current Location, or paste Coordinates like "38.57, -109.55".
  7. Add a Place Name if you like, such as "Gas station on Main St".
  8. Under The Route, tap Mapped (you draw the route or import a GPX file next) or Freeride (no set route, everyone follows the leader).
  9. Under Who's Riding, pick the Vehicles Welcome.
  10. Tap Create Public Ride or Create Private Ride.

From 2.1 a satellite map sits under Search a Place or Address, on every device. It opens near where you are. Tap it to drop the meeting point there, then drag the pin to fine-tune the spot. Searching a place, Use My Current Location and typed Coordinates move the pin too. (The map needs a signal; without one, set the point by search, location or coordinates as before.)

Found a park in Scout? Tap Create a Ride Here on its page: Create a Ride opens as a public ride with the meeting point and Place Name already set to the park. Move the pin if your group meets somewhere else, such as the park's gate or a gas station nearby.

Planning a rally or a ride weekend with several rides? That's an event, not one ride. Tap Event or Public Ride? at the top of Create a Ride to see the difference, or see Events.

If you picked Mapped, the route builder opens right away so you can draw the trail. See Build a Route. Once the ride is saved you land on its page, ready to invite riders.

Ride One of Your Trails

This is a Pro feature. Open a trail in Navigate and tap Plan a Group Ride on This Trail. The new ride starts with the trail's name, the trail as its route and its start as the meeting point, under a Riding banner. Pick the day, change anything you like and create it.

If the trail has a width limit, the Riding banner shows it (and whether your Garage machines fit), and the ride's Instructions for Joined Riders start with a line like "Trail width limit: 50". Wider machines won't fit." so everyone who joins sees it. Edit or delete that line like any other.

Tip

Only riders who have joined see the exact meeting spot. Public pages show the general area, within about a mile.

A Mapped ride, where you draw the route, is a Pro feature. See Free and Pro. On a Free account, Mapped carries a Pro tag; pick Freeride to create the ride for free.

Pick the Day

Under When, tap Today, Tomorrow, Sat or Sun for the coming weekend. For any other day, tap Calendar.

Then either type the date or pick it on the calendar:

  • Type a Date: tap the box and type the month, day and year, such as 06/12/2027. The number pad is enough; the slashes go in for you. The calendar jumps to that day.
  • Tap a day: use the arrows to go to another month, or tap the month's name (such as "November 2026") to choose a year and month, then tap the day.

You can schedule a ride for any day from today up to three years out, so rides next year and the year after are fine. Past days and days further out are greyed out.

The day you picked shows under When, with how far away it is, such as "In 8 Weeks". Tap Done to fold the calendar away.

On a tablet, the calendar sits in the same form, with bigger day buttons.

Pick the Time

No typing needed. Meet At starts at 8:00 AM. To change it, tap the time, then:

  1. Tap AM or PM.
  2. Tap the hour, 1 to 12.
  3. Tap the minute, from :00 to :55 in 5-minute steps.

The time shows in big letters as you tap. Tap Done to fold the picker away.

Departure Time and Expected Finish (under Add Route, Difficulty and More) work the same way. They start as Not Set: tap Add and the app suggests a time (30 minutes after you meet for departure, 8 hours after for the finish), then tap to adjust. Tap Clear to leave one empty.

On a tablet or a computer, all 12 hours and all 12 minutes fit in one row each.

Public Ride Options

A public ride is listed in its state so riders can find it. The State fills in from the meeting point. If it's wrong (it can happen near a border), tap Change State and pick the right one.

Public rides also have a Joining choice:

  • Anyone Can Join
  • I Approve Riders

See Approvals and the Waitlist for how requests work. Private rides don't have this choice: anyone holding your invite link or code can join.

Limit Who Can Come

Under Who's Riding, set Max Vehicles, Max Riders or both. Leave them empty for no limit. When the ride is full, new riders go on a waitlist. See Approvals and the Waitlist.

Pick your own invite code. Under Invite Code, leave Your Own Code (Optional) empty and OnMyLead makes a code for you. Or type your own, like MOAB-2026: 6 to 20 letters and numbers, with dashes between words. A green Available means no other ride uses it. See Choose Your Own Code.

Invite a Riding Group

If you're in a riding group, an Invite a Group section shows up. Pick one or more groups under Your Riding Groups. Everyone in them gets a notification and sees the ride on their Rides tab, and they join with one tap. See Riding Groups.

Add More Details

Tap Add Route, Difficulty and More to fill in any of these:

  • Description
  • Departure Time and Expected Finish (used to check your ride against sunset, see Ride Day and Weather)
  • Destination, which anyone who can see the ride sees, with directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps
  • Route Length (Miles), used to warn riders whose fuel range is too short (see Fuel Range)
  • Difficulty: Easy, Moderate, Hard or Extreme
  • Experience: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced or Expert
  • What to Bring, Required Equipment and Fuel
  • Instructions for Joined Riders, which only riders on the ride see
  • Ride Bubble: Standard, Tight Trail, Desert or Highway (see Ride Bubble)

After You Create It

Your ride page opens with a card that has the invite code, a QR code and buttons to share it. See Invite Riders. You're also the ride's organizer and leader. See Organizer Controls.

How Many Rides You Can Create

Free accounts create one ride each month. Joining rides is always free and unlimited. On Free, the top of the Create a Ride screen shows Rides Created This Month so you know where you stand. When you've used this month's free ride, the app tells you the date your next one opens (the first of the month, UTC). Creating more rides than that is a Pro feature. See Free and Pro.

Note

A ride counts toward the month you create it, even if you later cancel or delete it. Copying a ride or using Ride Again also creates a new ride.

Pro bought on Android through Google Play lifts the monthly limit the same way, on every device you sign in to.

On Home, the Create a Ride card shows where you stand: 1 Left This Month or None Left This Month on Free, and Unlimited on Pro or the trial. On Free, tap the label to see what Pro adds.

Create a Ride for an Event

On an event you host, Group Rides > Add > Create a New Ride for This Event opens Create a Ride with a For Your Event note at the top. Once you create the ride, it shows under the event's Group Rides. See Events.
