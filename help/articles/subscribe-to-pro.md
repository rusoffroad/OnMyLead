OnMyLead Pro adds unlimited rides, offline maps, GPX import, saved riding groups and more. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro) for the full list.

## Prices

| Plan | US Price |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | $4.99 a month |
| **Annual** | $29.99 a year, about $2.50 a month. Saves about 50% compared with paying monthly. |

Outside the US, the app shows the price in your local currency.

## Subscribe

1. On the **You** tab, under **Settings**, tap **Subscription**.
2. Tap **Upgrade to Pro**. During your free trial, this button says **Keep Pro After the Trial**.
3. Choose **Annual** (marked **Best Value**) or **Monthly**.
4. Tap the **Subscribe for** button under the plans, for example **Subscribe for $29.99 a Year**.
5. Confirm the purchase in Apple's window.

On Android, step 5 is Google Play's window instead, and the payment goes to your Google Play account.

When it's done, the app says **You’re on OnMyLead Pro. Thanks for riding with us.** and your plan shows **Pro**.

You can also reach the Pro screen by tapping **See OnMyLead Pro** on any Pro prompt, or **Unlimited With Pro** on the Create a Ride screen.

:::note
You can subscribe in the OnMyLead app only, on iPhone, iPad or Android. The website can't sell subscriptions, but Pro you buy in the app also works when you use OnMyLead on the website.
:::

## Already Have Pro From the Other Store?

Pro belongs to your OnMyLead account, so a subscription from the App Store works on Android and one from Google Play works on iPhone and iPad. If you already pay for Pro through one store, the Pro screen on a device that uses the other store says so instead of showing the plans, so you aren't charged twice. To move your subscription to the other store, cancel it in the store you pay now and subscribe on the other device after it ends.

## Billing

- Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
- The subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
- You can manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. See [Manage or Cancel Your Subscription](/help/manage-subscription).
- On Android, payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it in Google Play, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
- The free Pro Trial for new accounts is separate. It needs no payment and never turns into a paid subscription on its own.

The Pro screen links to the **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **"Waiting on Apple to approve the purchase."** This happens with Ask to Buy or when your bank needs to check the payment. Once it's approved, Pro starts on its own.
- **"Waiting on Google Play to approve the purchase."** This happens when you pay with a method Google confirms later, such as cash at a store. Pro starts on its own once the payment goes through.
- **"The purchase didn’t go through. You weren’t charged."** Check your connection and try again.
- **Your plan still says Free after buying.** Make sure you have signal, then close and reopen the app. If it still says Free, tap **Restore Purchases**. See [Restore Purchases](/help/restore-purchases).
