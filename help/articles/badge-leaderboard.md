The **Leaderboard** ranks riders by the badges they earned by riding. Look at everyone, or narrow it to a country and then a state or province.

## Open the Leaderboard

- On **Badges**, tap **Leaderboard** under your rank.
- On **Home**, tap **Leaderboard** beside **Your Badges**.

You need to be signed in. It opens on the state or country you ride from, if you picked one.

## How Riders Are Ranked

- Each badge level you earn by riding counts once, the same as your rank: Bronze is 1, Silver 2, Gold 3, for up to 21 levels. See [Earn Badges and Ranks](/help/badges-and-ranks).
- Ties go to the rider with more Gold, then more Silver, then whoever reached that total first.
- **OnMyLead Founder** and **Brand Ambassador** don't count, but they still show beside the rider's name.

Each row shows the place, the rider's picture and name with their top badge, their rank, where they ride from, their total levels and how many Gold, Silver and Bronze they hold. Tap a rider to open their profile.

Your own row is highlighted. If you're further down than the list goes, your place shows at the bottom.

## Filter by Country and State

1. Under **Country**, tap **Everywhere** or a country.
2. For the United States, Canada, Mexico and Australia, a **State** (or **Province**) row appears. Tap one, or **All of** the country.

Only places with riders on the board are listed, plus your own.

## Pick Where You Ride

Riders show on a country or state board only after they pick where they ride from.

1. On the Leaderboard, tap **Where You Ride**.
2. On iPhone, iPad and Android, tap **Use My Location** to fill it in. Your phone works out the country and state itself, and only those two are saved, never where you are.
3. Or pick your country (type to search) and then your state or province.
4. Tap **Save**.

To take it off, open **Where You Ride** and tap **Remove My Area**. On the website, pick it from the list (there's no **Use My Location** there).

## Leave the Leaderboard

Turn off **Show Me on the Leaderboard**. You disappear from every board right away, and you can still look at it. Turn it back on any time.

If **Who Sees Your Activity** in your Feed settings is **Nobody**, you start off the leaderboard until you turn **Show Me** on.

Riders you've blocked, or who blocked you, never see each other on the leaderboard. See [What Other Riders Can See](/help/privacy).

## On a Tablet

On an iPad or Android tablet, and on a wide window on the website, your place, **Where You Ride**, **Show Me** and the filters sit in a column beside the board.
