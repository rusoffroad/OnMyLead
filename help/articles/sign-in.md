You need an account to create rides, keep a Garage and save riding groups. You can sign in with a social account, with your email and a password, or with a link we email you.

## Open the Sign-In Screen

1. On the **Rides** tab or the **You** tab, tap **Sign In or Create a Free Account**.
2. At the top, choose **Sign In** if you already have an account, or **Create Account** if you're new.

Tapping something that needs an account, like the **Groups** tab, also opens this screen.

## Sign In With Apple, Google or Facebook

Depending on what's turned on, you'll see **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Facebook** at the top of the screen.

1. Tap the button for the account you want to use.
2. Follow the steps on the Apple, Google or Facebook screen.
3. You're signed in and taken back to OnMyLead. If you were new, your account is created.

With Apple, the name on your Apple Account is used as your ride name the first time you sign in, if Apple shares it. You can change it any time. See [Your Ride Name and Profile](/help/ride-name-and-profile).

:::note
On the website, **Continue with Apple** may not be there. Use Apple in the iPhone app, or sign in on the website another way.
:::

## Create an Account With Email and a Password

1. Tap **Create Account** at the top.
2. Enter **Your Name**. This is what riders will see, and you can change it later.
3. Enter your **Email**.
4. Enter a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
5. Tap **Create Account**.
6. If the app says **Almost done**, open the email we sent and tap the link on the same iPhone. Your email is confirmed and you're signed in.

## Sign In With Email and a Password

1. Tap **Sign In** at the top.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

## Sign In With an Email Link (No Password)

1. Enter your **Email**.
2. Tap **Or email me a sign-in link instead (no password)**.
3. Open the email on the same iPhone and tap the link.

The link signs you in. If you don't have an account yet, it creates one.

## Reset a Forgotten Password

1. Tap **Sign In** at the top.
2. Enter your **Email**.
3. Tap **Forgot your password? Email me a reset link**.
4. Open the email on your iPhone and tap the link.
5. Enter a **New Password** of at least 8 characters.
6. Tap **Save Password**.

If you see **This Link Has Expired**, tap **Back to Sign In** and ask for a new reset link.

## Opening an Email Link on Another Device

If you tap a link from us in a browser on an iPhone, you may see **Open OnMyLead**. Tap **Open in the App** to finish in the app, or **Continue on the Website** to stay in the browser.

If you see **Your Email Is Confirmed** and a request to sign in again, the link opened somewhere other than where you signed up. Your email is confirmed. Tap **Sign In** and sign in once more with your email and password.

## Sign Out

1. Go to the **You** tab.
2. Scroll down and tap **Sign Out**.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **That email and password don't match**: check for typos, or use the reset link above.
- **Please confirm your email first**: tap the link in the email we sent you, then sign in.
- **There's already an account with that email**: switch to **Sign In** at the top.
- **Too many emails sent just now**: wait a few minutes and try again.
- **That Link Didn't Work**: the link expired or was already used. Tap **Back to Sign In** and ask for a new one.

Still stuck? [Contact Us](/help/contact-support) and pick **Account and Sign-In**.
