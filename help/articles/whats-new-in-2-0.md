OnMyLead 2.0 is the biggest update yet. It works the same on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets. Here are the big changes, with the guide for each. The full list is on the [Release Notes](/help/release-notes) page.

## The Big Changes

- **A new Home.** Join a Ride sits at the top with a box for the invite code, with Create a Ride and Find a Ride under it, then your next ride, your riding group, public rides near you and a Feed of your friends' rides. See [Your Home Screen](/help/home-screen).
- **Map Mode** (it was called Navigate). Your offroad map for when it's just you: speed, elevation, heading and trip, custom trails you draw yourself, Track Line of where you've been and named spots like water crossings. See [Navigate Offroad and Map Your Own Trails](/help/navigation-mode).
- **Tilt Meter and Crash Alert.** See your roll and pitch over the map and get a warning before a steep lean. In Ride Mode, a hard hit or a rollover starts an I'm OK countdown, and if you don't answer, your group gets an EMERGENCY alert. It never calls 911. See [Use the Tilt Meter and Crash Alert](/help/tilt-and-crash-alert).
- **Events.** Rallies, poker runs and ride weekends on one page with the agenda and the group rides to join. See [Events](/help/events).
- **Social.** Like and comment on your friends' rides, badges and trails, and post ride reports with a photo. See [Like, Comment and Post in Social](/help/social).
- **Badges.** Earn Bronze, Silver and Gold and climb from Rookie to Legend, plus the OnMyLead Founder and Brand Ambassador badges. See [Earn Badges and Ranks](/help/badges-and-ranks).
- **Light mode and themes.** Dark, Light or Match Phone, four themes and Color Blind Mode. See [Change the Look](/help/appearance).
- **12 languages.** See [Change the App's Language](/help/language).
- **Sync across devices.** Your trails and settings follow you to every phone, tablet and the website you sign in on. See [Use OnMyLead on More Than One Device](/help/sync-across-devices).
- **A smoother Ride Mode,** with the leader line in order from the leader to the tail gunner, your own typed messages, Full Map, Follow Me and Music Controls. See [Use Ride Mode](/help/ride-mode).
- **Invite Friends.** When a rider you invite finishes their first ride, you both save 50% on your first year of Pro Annual. See [Invite Friends and Get 50% Off Pro](/help/refer-a-friend).
- **New Free and Pro plans.** Joining rides, following the leader, Ride Mode and every safety feature stay free, and Map Mode is free for 8 hours a month. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).

## Get the Update

Open the App Store on iPhone or iPad, or Google Play on Android, and update OnMyLead. Everything you had before, your rides, groups, garage and trails, is still there.
