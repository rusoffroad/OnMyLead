The Welcome Tour is a short run of cards that shows you around OnMyLead: joining and creating rides, the live map in Ride Mode, status taps and EMERGENCY, sharing with no service, Map Mode, Out Riding, Places to Ride and Events, Social, badges and Starlink. It takes under a minute.

## When It Shows

- **New to OnMyLead:** the tour opens by itself on Home the first time you open the app, after you sign in and agree to the Rider Safety and Liability Agreement.
- **Had the app before 4.0:** you see one **What's New in 4.0** card instead. Tap **Take the Tour** on it for the whole tour, or **Got It** to close it.

It shows once per account. If you've seen it on your phone, it won't show again on your iPad or on onmylead.com.

Cards only appear for features you can use. For example, the weather card shows once weather is available in the app.

Cards for Pro features show a **Pro** tag above the title if you aren't on Pro.

## Move Through the Tour

1. Tap **Next** (or swipe left) for the next card, and **Back** (or swipe right) to go back.
2. Tap **Skip** in the corner to close it at any time.
3. On the last card, tap **Let's Ride** to go to Home.

The trail of dots above the buttons shows how far along you are.

On a tablet, the tour shows as a card over Home: the picture is beside the words in landscape and above them in portrait. On onmylead.com, the arrow keys move between cards and Esc closes the tour.

## Take the Tour Again

1. Tap **You**.
2. Under **Help**, tap **Take the Tour**.
