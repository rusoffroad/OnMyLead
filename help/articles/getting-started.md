OnMyLead is a group ride app for off-road riders. The leader creates a ride, the crew joins with a code, link or QR code, and on the trail everyone shares one live map, a group chat and quick taps like **Need Fuel** and **Flat Tire** that tell the whole group at once.

## Get the App

OnMyLead is an iPhone app. Download **OnMyLead** from the App Store on your iPhone, then open it.

:::note
OnMyLead is made for iPhone. There is no iPad version.
:::

## Make Your Account

You can look around without an account, but you need one to create rides, save your Garage and keep riding groups.

1. On the **Rides** tab, tap **Sign In or Create a Free Account**.
2. Tap **Create Account** at the top.
3. Use one of the **Continue with** buttons, or enter **Your Name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters, then tap **Create Account**.
4. If we email you a link, tap it on the same iPhone to confirm your email and finish signing in.

See [Sign In and Create an Account](/help/sign-in) for every way to sign in.

Next, pick your ride name. It's what other riders see on the map, the rider list and in chat. Until you pick one, Home shows **Pick Your Ride Name** and other riders see you as Rider. See [Your Ride Name and Profile](/help/ride-name-and-profile).

New accounts start with a free Pro Trial. See [The Pro Trial](/help/pro-trial).

## Find Your Way Around

Four tabs run along the bottom of the screen.

### Rides

Your home screen.

- **Create a Ride**: start a ride that you lead. Free accounts create one ride a month. See [Create a Ride](/help/create-a-ride) and [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).
- **Join a Ride**: type an invite code and tap **Open**. See [Join a Ride](/help/join-a-ride).
- **Find a Ride**: opens the Find tab.
- When one of your rides is live, a card at the top says **Your ride is live. Tap to open Ride Mode**.
- **Ride Invites**, **Your Rides** and **Past Rides** list the rides you're on. Swipe a ride left to delete it (if you organize it) or leave it. The app always asks first.
- **Public Rides** shows a few upcoming public rides near the bottom.

### Find

Public rides on a map. Choose **Near Me** or **By State**, or use **Search Near a Place** to look around a trailhead, park or town. See [Find a Ride](/help/find-a-ride).

### Groups

Your riding groups, the groups you're in, and **Leaders You Follow**. Tap **New Group** to save your crew. You need an account for this tab; if you're signed out, it takes you to sign in. See [Riding Groups](/help/riding-groups).

### You

Everything that isn't a ride:

- Your ride name and plan at the top.
- **Your Gear**: [Garage](/help/garage), [Trip Planner](/help/trip-planner) and [Offline Maps](/help/offline-maps).
- **On the Trail**: **Share My Location** and [Starlink](/help/starlink).
- **Settings**: [Notifications](/help/notification-settings), **Directions** (which maps app **Navigate** opens), **Subscription** and **Blocked Riders**.
- **Account**: the email you sign in with.
- **Help**: **Contact Us** and **Help and Support**.
- **Sign Out** and **Delete Account** at the bottom.

## Permissions the App Asks For

### Notifications

After you sign in, your iPhone asks if OnMyLead can send notifications. Say yes so you hear when someone in your group taps **Need Help** or **EMERGENCY**, even with the phone in your pocket. You choose everything else in [Notification Settings](/help/notification-settings).

### Location

OnMyLead asks for your location when you first use it, for example to find rides near you or when you start sharing on a ride. Your location is shared only with riders you choose, only while you're sharing.

When you share on a ride, your iPhone may also ask to allow location **Always**. Allow it so your group keeps seeing you while your phone is locked on the mount. Sharing always stops when your timer ends or the ride ends. See [Location Sharing](/help/location-sharing).

### Photos

If you add a photo of your machine to your Garage, the app asks to use your photos. Only the photo you pick is uploaded.

## Using the Website

Some things also work at onmylead.com in a web browser:

- Sign in with the same account.
- Create a ride from your computer and draw its route on a big map. See [Create a Ride on the Website](/help/create-a-ride-on-the-website).
- Open a ride with its invite code.
- Send us a message with **Contact Us**.

Ride Mode and live tracking stay in the iPhone app.

## Notices From the Team

Sometimes a card at the top of Home shows a message from the OnMyLead team, like **Planned Maintenance** or an announcement. You can close an announcement with its X. A maintenance notice stays until the work is done.

If a screen says something like **Trip Planner Is Off for Now**, that feature is switched off for a while. Everything you saved is kept, and your rides work as usual.
