**Social** is where you see everything your friends and the riders you follow have been up to: finished rides, badges, shared trails, group rides and posts. You can like anything, read every comment, and open any rider's profile.

## Open Social

- On the **Rides** tab, tap **Open Social** next to **Feed**, or tap any post in the feed.
- Or tap **Friends & Social** in your toolkit.

On a phone, Social has three tabs: **Activity** (everyone's posts), **Friends** and **My Posts**. On an iPad, an Android tablet or a big window, your friends sit on the left and the posts on the right, so you can switch without losing your place.

## Like and Comment

- Tap the **heart** under a post to like it. Tap it again to take the like back.
- Tap **Comment**, or tap the post, to open it on its own page with every comment and who liked it.
- Type in the box at the bottom (up to 500 characters) and tap the red arrow.

You can delete your own comments. You can also delete any comment on your own posts, rides, badges and trails.

## Post on Your Profile

1. In Social, tap **Post a ride report…** at the top (it's also at the top of your own profile).
2. Write up to 500 characters about the ride, the trail or the crew.
3. Tap **Take Photo** or **Add Photo** to add one picture, if you like.
4. Tap **Post**.

Your post shows on your profile and in the feeds of the riders your **Who Sees Your Activity** setting allows (see [Your Home Screen](/help/home-screen)). To delete it, tap **⋯** on the post, then **Delete Post**. Its likes and comments go with it.

Photos are made smaller before they upload: no more than 1,280 pixels on the long side and under 1 MB. Nothing about where you took the photo is added.

## Rider Profiles

Tap a rider's name anywhere in Social to open their profile: their picture, the **PRO** tag if they have OnMyLead Pro, when they joined, their rides, miles and badges, and their posts, rides, badges and trails, newest first. Tap the picture to see it full size.

From a profile you can **Message** a friend, **Add Friend**, or **Report** them. You see a rider's activity only if their **Who Sees Your Activity** setting allows it.

## See a Picture Full Size

Tap any profile picture, in Social, Friends, Messages, chats or Who's Riding, to see it big. Tap a post's photo to see the whole photo. Tap anywhere to close it.

## Share to Facebook, Instagram or X

1. Tap **Share** under one of your own rides, badges or posts, or under a shared trail.
2. Tap **Share Picture** to share a picture card with the miles, badge or trail name, or **Share Text Only**.
3. Pick Facebook, Instagram, X, Messages or any other app.

Every share links to onmylead.com. Your location is never in a share, and you can't share other riders' rides or posts, or who's out riding right now.

On the website, **Share Picture** opens your browser's share menu. If your browser doesn't have one, the picture downloads and the text is copied so you can paste it into your post.

## Free and Pro

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| See Social and every comment | Yes | Yes |
| Like posts | Yes | Yes |
| Comment | No | Yes |
| Post on your profile | No | Yes |
| Share to Facebook, Instagram and X | No | Yes |

Riders with Pro (or the Pro Trial) show a gold **PRO** tag next to their name. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).

## Keep It Friendly

If a post or comment shouldn't be there, tap **⋯** on the post (or **Report** under a comment) and pick a reason. A post or comment that several riders report is hidden until the OnMyLead team looks at it, and the team removes ones that break the rules. Blocked riders never see your posts or comments, and you never see theirs. See [Report or Block a Rider](/help/report-and-block).

To stop likes and comments notifications, turn off **Likes and Comments** in [Notification Settings](/help/notification-settings).
