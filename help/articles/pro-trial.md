When you make a new OnMyLead account, you get 30 days of OnMyLead Pro for free. It starts on its own. There's nothing to sign up for and no payment details to enter.

:::tip
You're never charged when the trial ends. It does not turn into a paid subscription. Your account simply moves to Free unless you choose to subscribe.
:::

:::release 2.1
## A Fresh Trial With 2.1

OnMyLead 2.1 makes a few more things Pro: Bluetooth Sharing with no service, CarPlay, customizing your dot and group photos. So nobody loses anything the day it arrives, every rider who doesn't have Pro gets a fresh 30-day Pro trial when 2.1 comes out, even if you already had a trial or are partway through one. Riders who subscribe, or have Pro from OnMyLead, keep it as before.

While a trial runs, Home shows **Your Pro Trial Runs Until** and the date. Close it with the **×**; it comes back only if your trial's end date changes.
:::

## See How Many Days Are Left

On the **You** tab, under **Settings**, tap **Subscription**. **Your Plan** shows **Pro Trial** with the days remaining, the date it ends, and a row of 30 marks that fills in as the days go by.

The trial is the same on Android. It belongs to your account, not your phone, so it shows the same days left on every device you sign in to.

## Trial Reminders

OnMyLead reminds you a week, 3 days and a day before your trial ends, and again when it ends.

:::until 1.0.3
- To turn off the notifications, switch off **Pro Trial Reminders** in [Notification Settings](/help/notification-settings).
:::
:::release 1.0.3
- To turn off the notifications, switch off **Badges and Pro Trial** in [Notification Settings](/help/notification-settings). That also stops badge notifications and the notice when a trail you shared is hidden.
:::
- The same reminders come by email too. Switch off **Email Me Too** to stop the emails.

## When the Trial Ends

Your account moves to Free. Nothing you made during the trial is deleted:

- Your rides, routes, groups and downloaded maps stay yours.
- New Pro actions, like downloading another map area or importing a GPX file, need Pro again.
- Free accounts create one ride a month. Rides you created earlier in the same month, during the trial, count toward that month.

See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro) for exactly what changes.

## Keep Pro After the Trial

1. On the **You** tab, under **Settings**, tap **Subscription**.
2. Tap **Keep Pro After the Trial**.
3. Choose **Annual** or **Monthly**.
4. Tap the **Subscribe for** button under the plans and confirm with Apple.

Apple charges you when you confirm, and your paid subscription starts right away. See [Subscribe to Pro](/help/subscribe-to-pro).
