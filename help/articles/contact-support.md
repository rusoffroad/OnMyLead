Questions, problems or ideas for OnMyLead go straight to the team at RUS Offroad. A real person reads every message.

:::warning
OnMyLead is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number first.
:::

## Send a Message From the App

:::until 3.0
1. Go to the **You** tab.
:::
:::release 3.0
1. Go to **You** (your picture at the top of Home).
:::
2. Under **Help**, tap **Contact Us**.
3. Under **What's It About?**, pick a topic:
   - **Something Isn't Working**
   - **Feature Request**
   - **Billing and Pro**
   - **Account and Sign-In**
   - **Safety or a Rider**
   - **General Feedback**
   - **Something Else**
4. Check **Your Name** (optional) and **Your Email**. If you're signed in, they're filled in for you.
5. Write **Your Message**. The hint under the box suggests what to include for your topic.
6. Tap **Send Message**.

You'll see **Message Sent**. We reply to the email you gave, usually within a day.

:::release 1.0.1
## Report a Bug

If something in the app isn't working, tell us with a screenshot so we can fix it fast.

1. Take a screenshot of the problem:
   - **iPhone**: press the side button and volume up together.
   - **iPad**: press the top button and a volume button together.
   - **Android phone or tablet**: press the power and volume down buttons together.
:::until 3.0
2. Go to the **You** tab. Under **Help**, tap **Report a Bug**. The form opens with **Something Isn't Working** already picked.
:::
:::release 3.0
2. Go to **You** (your picture at the top of Home). Under **Help**, tap **Report a Bug**. The form opens with **Something Isn't Working** already picked.
:::
3. Tap **Add a Screenshot** and pick your screenshot. Tap **Change Screenshot** or **Remove** if you picked the wrong one.

:::release 3.0
   On the **Crop the Screenshot** screen, zoom in on the part that shows the problem (or leave it whole), then tap **Use Photo**.
:::

4. Write what you tapped, what happened and what you expected, then tap **Send Message**.

Your report also includes your phone model, your OnMyLead version, the screen you were on before you opened the form, and a short log of what the app just did (the screens you opened, errors and requests that failed). The log never includes your location, your messages or anything you typed, and it's kept only in memory: it's gone when you close the app.

On onmylead.com/contact, pick **Something Isn't Working** to add a screenshot the same way. Your browser goes with the report instead of a phone model.
:::

## Send a Message From the Website

Go to onmylead.com/contact. The same form works there, and you don't need to sign in.

## Email Us

You can also email support@rusoffroad.com.

## Tips for a Quick Answer

- **Something Isn't Working**: tell us what you tapped, what happened and what you expected. The ride name helps if it's about a ride.
- **Billing and Pro**: purchases are billed by Apple, and refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com. We're still glad to help.

On Android, the hint says purchases are billed by Google Play, with refunds through your Google Play order history.
- **Account and Sign-In**: if you sign in with a different email than the one on the form, tell us which.
- **Safety or a Rider**: to report a rider, you can also press and hold their message in a ride. See [Report or Block a Rider](/help/report-and-block).

## Open These Guides From the App

The step-by-step guides you're reading now (onmylead.com/help) are one tap away in the app:

:::until 3.0
- On the **You** tab, under **Help**, tap **Help Center: How-To Guides**.
:::
:::release 3.0
- On **You** (your picture at the top of Home), under **Help**, tap **Help Center: How-To Guides**.
:::
:::until 3.0
- Or tap **Help & Support** on the **Home** tab, or **Help and Support** on the **You** tab, then tap **Help Center: How-To Guides** at the top.
:::
:::release 3.0
- Or tap **Help & Support** on the **Home** tab, or **Help and Support** on **You** (your picture at the top of Home), then tap **Help Center: How-To Guides** at the top.
:::

The guides open inside OnMyLead. Tap **Done** (iPhone and iPad) or the close button at the top (Android phones and tablets) to go back to the app. On onmylead.com, the link opens the Help Center in the same tab.
:::

In the Android app, those answers give the Android steps where they differ, such as allowing location **While Using the App**.
