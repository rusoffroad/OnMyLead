The Trip Planner keeps a packing list for each trip. Tick items off as they go in the rig, and reuse the list next time.

## Open the Trip Planner

1. Go to the **You** tab.
2. Under **Your Gear**, tap **Trip Planner**.

You need to be signed in.

## Plan a New Trip

1. Tap **Plan a New Trip**.
2. Enter a **Trip Name**, like "Moab long weekend".
3. Add dates if you like: **Starts** and **Ends**, written like 2026-10-12. **Ends** is optional.
4. If you have machines in your [Garage](/help/garage), pick the one you're **Taking**.
5. Tap **Create Trip**.

:::note
Free accounts plan one trip at a time. A trip counts until its last day has passed. Planning several trips at once is part of Pro. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).
:::

## Add Items

1. On the trip, tap **Add an Item**.
2. Enter the **Item**, like "Tow strap".
3. Set **How Many** with the number box or the minus and plus buttons.
4. Pick a **Category**, like **Recovery**, **First Aid**, **Camping** or **Food & Water**.
5. Add **Notes** if you like, such as size or where it lives in the rig.
6. Tap **Add**.

Items are grouped by category, each showing how many are packed.

## Start From a Starter List

Tap **Add a Starter List** and pick one, like **Day Ride**, **Overnight / Camping**, **Desert**, **Mountain / Snow**, **Recovery Kit**, **First Aid**, **Tools & Spares** or **Starlink / Power**. One tap adds the items. You can add more than one list, and nothing is added twice.

This is a Pro feature. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).

## Tick Items Off

Tap anywhere on an item's row to check it off. Tap again to uncheck it. The bar at the top shows how much is packed. When everything is checked, it turns green and says so, like All 24 packed.

To change or remove an item, tap **Edit** on its row, then tap **Save** or **Remove Item**.

## After the Trip

Under **After the Trip**:

- **Uncheck All** puts every item back to not packed. The list stays.
- **Copy to a New Trip** starts a new trip with the same list. This is a Pro feature. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).

## Link a Trip to a Ride

When you create or edit a trip, you can pick a ride you've joined under **For a Ride**. The trip then shows a link to that ride.

This is a Pro feature. See [Free and Pro](/help/free-vs-pro).

## Edit or Delete a Trip

1. Open the trip.
2. Tap **Edit Trip** to change the name, dates, machine or ride, then tap **Save**.
3. Or tap **Delete**, then confirm. The trip and all its items are removed.

## Gear Suggestions

A trip's page may show **Gear From RUS Offroad**, with products that fit your list and machine. See [Gear From RUS Offroad](/help/rus-gear).
