OnMyLead promo codes are Apple offer codes for OnMyLead Pro. You redeem them through Apple, right from the app.

## Redeem a Code in the App

1. On the **You** tab, under **Settings**, tap **Subscription**.
2. Tap **Redeem Promo Code**.
3. Enter your code in Apple's window and follow the steps to confirm.

Apple shows what the code gives you before you confirm. Once Apple accepts the code, OnMyLead checks it and your plan changes to **Pro**. This can take a moment.

You can also tap **Redeem Promo Code** at the bottom of the Pro screen.

## Redeemed It in the App Store Instead?

That works too. Open OnMyLead afterwards, signed in to your account, with signal. The app picks up the code on its own and turns on Pro.

## Good to Know

- A promo code becomes an OnMyLead Pro subscription with your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it like any subscription. See [Manage or Cancel Your Subscription](/help/manage-subscription).
- Promo codes can only be redeemed in the iPhone app, not on the website.
- If your plan doesn't change to Pro, make sure you have signal and are signed in to the right OnMyLead account, then tap **Restore Purchases**. See [Restore Purchases](/help/restore-purchases).
