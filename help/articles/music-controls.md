Music Controls let you pause, skip and turn the music up or down without switching apps, so the group map stays on your screen. They control the music already playing on your phone, from the app you play it in.

## Open Music Controls

1. In **Ride Mode** or **Navigate**, tap the round **Music** button with the music note. It sits on the right, just above **Center on Me**.
2. The Music sheet shows what's playing and which app it's in, with big **Previous Song**, **Play** or **Pause** and **Next Song** buttons, and the volume with **−** and **+**.
3. Swipe the sheet down or tap **Close** to go back to the map.

In [Full Map](/help/full-map), the Music button sits in the bar at the bottom, between **Controls** and **EMERGENCY**.

The buttons are big enough to use with gloves on, but pull over before you look at your phone.

## What Works on Your Phone

| | Play, Pause and Skip | Volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Android phone or tablet** | Any music or podcast app: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Pandora, Amazon Music and more | Yes |
| **iPhone or iPad** | Apple Music | Yes, for any app |
| **onmylead.com** | No | No |

**On iPhone and iPad,** Apple lets other apps control only Apple Music. When Spotify or another app is playing, the Music sheet says **Playing in Another App** and gives you the volume, plus **Open Spotify** if Spotify is on your phone (that leaves OnMyLead). Use the buttons on your headset or helmet communicator to skip. When nothing is playing, **Play** starts Apple Music.

**On Android,** the buttons control whichever app last played music. To see the song name and the app's name, tap **Show Song Names**, then switch on **OnMyLead Music Controls** under Notification Access in Settings. Android shares what's playing only with apps you allow there. OnMyLead doesn't read your notifications, and the buttons work without it.

**On iPhone,** tapping **Show Song Names** asks to let OnMyLead see the Apple Music song. If you said no before, it opens OnMyLead in Settings, where you can switch on **Media & Apple Music**.

## Hide the Music Button

Don't want it? Tap **Hide Music Button** at the bottom of the Music sheet, or go to the **You** tab and switch off **Music Controls**. The button disappears from Ride Mode and Navigate. Switch **Music Controls** back on in the **You** tab any time. The setting is kept on each phone or tablet separately.

:::note
What you listen to stays on your phone. OnMyLead doesn't send it to the server or show it to other riders.
:::
