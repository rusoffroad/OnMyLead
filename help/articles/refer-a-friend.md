Invite a riding buddy who's new to OnMyLead. When they finish their first ride, you both get **Pro Annual for $14.99 your first year** instead of $29.99. The discount comes from the App Store or Google Play, so it's billed the same way as any Pro subscription.

## Invite a Friend

1. On the **You** tab, under **Settings**, tap **Invite Friends**. You can also tap **Invite Friends, Both Get 50% Off** at the bottom of the Pro screen.
2. Tap **Share Invite** and send it, or tap **Copy Link**.

Your invite link is your friend link, so your friend can also add you as a friend. See [Add Friends](/help/friends).

## What Your Friend Does

1. Opens your link and creates a new OnMyLead account within 7 days.
2. Finishes a ride: leads one or joins one that runs for at least 10 minutes and is ended.

That's it. You both get a notification in the inbox, and the discount shows on **Invite Friends** and on the Pro screen.

## Claim Your Discount

1. On **Invite Friends** (or the Pro screen), find **You Earned 50% Off Pro Annual**.
2. Tap **Claim 50% Off**.

On iPhone and iPad, the App Store opens with your personal offer code filled in. Confirm it there. If the App Store doesn't open, the code is shown on the card: tap **Redeem Your Code** to try again.

On an Android phone or tablet, Google Play opens Pro Annual at the discounted price. Confirm the purchase there.

Once the store confirms it, your plan changes to **Pro**. The first year costs $14.99. After that, Pro Annual renews at the regular price unless you cancel. See [Manage Your Subscription](/help/manage-subscription).

### Already Paying for Pro

- **On Google Play:** Google Play offers are for new subscriptions only, so instead your next bill moves back 6 months. Tap **Apply Your Reward**.
- **On the App Store:** your code is used at your next renewal. A monthly plan moves up to Pro Annual at half price.

On the website, the card says to claim the discount in the app.

## The Fine Print

- Each rider can earn the discount once. Your other invites still show under **Your Invites**.
- Your friend's account has to be new. Riders who already had an account can't be invited.
- You have 6 months to claim the discount.
- A discount you claimed in one store works in that store only. An Apple code is redeemed on an iPhone or iPad, and the Google Play offer on Android.
- If you see **Invites aren't open right now**, invites are paused. A discount you already earned can still be claimed.
