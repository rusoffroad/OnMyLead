Looking for people to ride with? The **Find** tab shows public rides near you or anywhere in a state. Private rides never show here. You need an invite link or code for those. See [Join a Ride](/help/join-a-ride).

![Find a Ride, near you or by state](/help/img/find.webp)

## Find Rides Near You

1. Tap the **Find** tab (or **Find a Ride** on the **Rides** tab).
2. Make sure **Near Me** is selected at the top.
3. Allow location if asked. If location is off, tap **Use My Location**, or pick your state instead.
4. Under **How Far Will You Go?**, pick **25 Miles**, **50 Miles**, **100 Miles** or **250 Miles**.

Rides show on the map and in a list under it, nearest first, with how far away each one is.

## Find Rides Near a Place

Planning a trip somewhere else? Search a park, trailhead or town.

1. On the **Find** tab, tap **Search Near a Place**.
2. Type the place and pick it from the results.

The list shows **Rides Near** that place. Tap **Back to Near Me** to go back to rides around you.

## Find Rides in a State

1. On the **Find** tab, tap **By State**.
2. Pick a state.

Rides in that state show soonest first. To switch states, tap the state's name in the corner of the map.

## Use the Map

- Each dot is a ride. Blue dots are upcoming rides. Red, glowing dots are live now.
- Tap a dot to see the ride's name, time and distance, then tap **Open Ride**.
- Move or zoom the map, then tap **Search This Area** to see rides there. To return, tap the button under the map that starts with **Back to**, such as **Back to Rides Near Me**.
- Use the **+** and **-** buttons to zoom, and the location button to recenter the map.
- On a wide screen, such as the website on a computer, the search and the list of rides sit on the left and the map fills the rest of the screen.

:::note
Dots sit on the ride's general meeting area, within about a mile. The exact meeting spot is shown only to riders who join.
:::

## What You'll See

Find shows public rides that are coming up or live right now. Each ride in the list shows its date, time, general area, difficulty, distance and the vehicles welcome. Tap a ride to open its page and join. See [Join a Ride](/help/join-a-ride).

If there are no rides yet, tap **Create a Public Ride** to start one. See [Create a Ride](/help/create-a-ride).

:::tip
The **Rides** tab also lists a few upcoming **Public Rides**. Tap **Find Rides on the Map** (or **See All on the Map**, which shows how many there are) to see them all.
:::
