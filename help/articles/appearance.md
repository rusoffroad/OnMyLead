OnMyLead starts in the dark Night Navy look. Go to **You** > **Appearance** to change it. The app redraws as soon as you tap, and it opens the same way next time.

## Mode

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dark** | The dark look OnMyLead has always had. The default. |
| **Light** | Light backgrounds with dark text. Easier to read in bright sun. |
| **Match Phone** | Follows your phone's own Dark Mode setting and switches when your phone does. |

## Themes

Each theme comes in a dark and a light version. Buttons stay logo red and links stay logo blue in every theme.

- **Night Navy**: the classic OnMyLead look.
- **Carbon**: true black. On phones with an OLED screen it uses a little less battery.
- **Desert**: warm sand and dust tones.
- **Forest**: deep pine green.

The maps look the same in every theme, and so do the name tags on the map, so riders always read them the same way.

## Color Blind Mode

Turn on **Color Blind Mode** if red and green (or blue and yellow) are hard to tell apart. It changes:

- Rider status colors: OK is sky blue, falling behind is yellow and separated is orange-red, and Ride Mode adds a symbol (✓, ! or ✕) next to a rider who needs a look.
- Trail ratings, trail spots (obstacles, hazards, water crossings and viewpoints) and your Track Line days.

The Appearance screen shows a preview of these colors as you switch.

:::tip
Changing the look only changes how OnMyLead looks on your screen. Other riders don't see your choice.
:::

## On a Tablet

On iPad and Android tablets the four themes sit side by side in one row. Everything else works the same as on a phone.
