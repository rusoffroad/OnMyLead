Gear From RUS Offroad

Why you may see product suggestions from RUS Offroad on your machine's page and your trip packing list, and how they're picked.

iPhone AppLast Updated October 14, 2026

On This Page

  1. Where You'll See It
  2. Buy or Browse
  3. When It Doesn't Show

OnMyLead is made by RUS Offroad. In a couple of places, the app may suggest RUS Offroad products that fit your machine or your packing list. The section is always labeled Gear From RUS Offroad.

Where You'll See It

  • On one of your machines in your Garage, with products that fit that machine.
  • On a trip in the Trip Planner, with products that fit your packing list and the machine you're taking.

Each suggestion shows the product, its price and how it fits, like "Fits your" followed by your machine, or "Fits most machines."

Buy or Browse

  • Tap Buy to open that product on the RUS Offroad store inside the app.
  • Tap Shop more at rusoffroad.com to browse the whole store.

Purchases happen on the RUS Offroad website, not through OnMyLead or your Apple subscription.

When It Doesn't Show

The section only appears when something fits. You won't see it if nothing matches, if the store can't be reached, or if suggestions are switched off for a while.
