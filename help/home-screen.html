Your Home Screen

What's on your home screen. Map Mode, joining and starting rides, events, who's out riding, your rides, your group and your feed, and how to customize it.

iPhone AppLast Updated November 14, 2026

Home is the first tab. It opens on a satellite map of your area with your greeting. Under it are the four things riders do most: Map Mode, Join a Ride, Start a Ride and Events. Below those are the sections you pick, like who's out riding and what's up next.

At the Top

  • Good Morning, Good Afternoon or Good Evening with your ride name, over a satellite map of your area. If you haven't allowed location, the map shows a riding area instead. The home screen never asks for your location.
  • Your badges, just above the greeting: your patches and your rank (like Veteran), with New Badge when you've earned one you haven't looked at. Tap them to open Badges, where Leaderboard shows how you rank. See Earn Badges and Ranks.
  • The bell opens your notifications, with a red number for new ones. See See and Clear Your Notifications.
  • Your picture (or your first letter) opens You: your profile, settings, Garage, Offline Maps and the rest.
  • When you're signed out, a flag lets you pick the app's language. Signed in, it's in You. See Change the App's Language.

Map Mode

The first card, over the bottom of the map. It says Ride Solo, because Map Mode is your own offroad map: nobody else sees where you are. When location is allowed it shows how many rider trails are near you.

Tap Open Map Mode, or the blue round Map Mode key in the middle of the tab bar. Recording, Track Line, drawing a custom trail and offline maps are all inside Map Mode. Pro riders can turn a solo ride into a group ride from there with Make It a Group Ride. See Navigate Offroad and Map Your Own Trails.

Join a Ride

The big red card. Type the invite code from the ride leader and tap Join. You can also open a ride's link, or scan its QR code with your phone's camera (the QR button on the card explains how). Joining is Unlimited on every plan. See Join a Ride.

Start a Ride and Events

Two tiles side by side:

  • Start a Ride creates a new ride. On Free it says how many you have left this month (1 Left This Month, or None Left This Month, which opens Pro). On Pro and during the trial it says Lead your group. See Create a Ride and Free and Pro.
  • Events opens rallies, poker runs and ride weekends. See Events.

When one of your rides is live, a card under them says Your ride is live. Tap to open Ride Mode.

Starlink

When your phone or tablet is on a Starlink's Wi-Fi, a Starlink card shows under those tiles with whether the dish is Online, Down and Up speeds, Latency, Sky Blocked, and how many devices are connected to the Starlink. Tap it to open the Starlink page. When no Starlink is connected, the card isn't there.

  • iPhone and iPad: the card starts showing after you've connected to your dish once on the Starlink page and allowed local network access.
  • Android phones and tablets: it shows on its own when you join the Starlink's Wi-Fi.
  • Website: browsers can't reach the dish, so there's no card.

Your Sections

These show by default, in this order:

  • Out Riding Now: friends who are out riding right now, with the spot they named (never where they are). Tap one, or See All, for Who's Riding. When nobody is out, the card asks if you're heading out.
  • Up Next: ride invites from your riding groups, your next ride (tap All to see every ride you're on), and your next event, or the next public event near you.
  • Your Group: your riding group with the newest chat message, with Group Chat and Plan a Group Ride. With more than one group, All opens your groups.
  • Feed: what your friends, riders you follow and your riding groups have been up to. Friends out riding show in Out Riding Now instead, so they aren't listed twice. Customize changes what the feed shows; Open Social opens Social.

You can also turn on Public Rides Near You and Past Rides.

Your Toolkit is always at the bottom of Home, under your sections: shortcuts to Who's Riding, Friends & Social, Garage, Trip Planner, Offline Maps, Starlink, Battery Saver and Share Location.

Swipe a ride left to delete it (if you organize it) or leave it. On onmylead.com, click Delete or Leave at the end of the row. The app always asks first.

Customize Home

  1. Scroll to the bottom of Home and tap Customize Home.
  2. Turn each section on or off with its switch.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to move a section higher or lower.
  4. Tap Done.

Map Mode, Join a Ride, Start a Ride and Events (Events and Group Rides from 3.1) always show first, your badges stay at the top and Your Toolkit stays at the bottom. Reset to Default puts everything back. Your layout is saved to your account, so it's the same on your phone, tablet and the website.

Public Rides Near You

Upcoming public rides, nearest first, as cards you swipe sideways. Tap the distance chip (Within 500 mi at first) to pick 50 mi, 100 mi, 250 mi, 500 mi, 1,000 mi or Anywhere. Signed out, this is the section Home shows.

The Tab Bar

Home, Rides (public rides on the map), the blue Map Mode key, Events and Social. Your riding groups are in Social (tap Riding Groups) and on Home under Your Group.

Your App Version

The very bottom of Home shows which version of OnMyLead you have, like Version 3.0 (11). Tell us this number when you contact support.

On a Tablet

In landscape, and on a big window, Map Mode, Join a Ride, Start a Ride, Events and Customize Home are on the left and your sections, then Your Toolkit, are on the right, with the tabs in a rail down the left edge (You is at the bottom of the rail). In portrait it's one column, like a phone. See Use OnMyLead on a Tablet.

On the Website

When you're signed in at onmylead.com, Home is the same home screen.
