Crop a Photo

Frame any photo before it uploads. Drag and zoom it in the crop screen, pick a shape where you can, then tap Use Photo.

iPhone AppLast Updated November 10, 2026

On This Page

  1. Where It Opens
  2. Crop Your Photo
  3. Adding Several Photos
  4. Phones and Tablets
  5. On the Website

Every photo you add in OnMyLead opens on a crop screen first, so you choose what shows. It works the same on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and onmylead.com.

Where It Opens

PhotoFrame
Profile pictureSquare, with a circle showing how it looks next to your name
Group photoWide (16:9)
Machine photo in your Garage4:3
Post photo in SocialYour pick
Event photosYour pick, one photo at a time
Trail photosYour pick
Screenshot for a bug reportYour pick

Crop Your Photo

  1. Take or choose a photo as usual. The crop screen opens.
  2. Drag the photo to move it inside the frame.
  3. Pinch to zoom, or double-tap to zoom in on a spot (double-tap again to zoom back out). The − and + buttons zoom too.
  4. Where you can pick a shape, tap Original, Square, 4:3, 16:9 or 4:5. Original keeps the photo's own shape. 4:3 and 16:9 follow the way the photo was taken, so a tall photo gets a tall frame.
  5. Tap Use Photo. Reset puts the photo back the way it started, and Cancel adds nothing.

The photo always fills the frame, so there are never empty edges. After you tap Use Photo, the app makes the photo smaller before it uploads, as it did before.

Adding Several Photos

When you pick more than one event photo, the crop screen shows each in turn (Photo 1 of 4, and so on). Tap Use the Rest As They Are to skip cropping the ones left.

Phones and Tablets

On a phone held upright, the controls sit under the photo. On a tablet, or a phone turned sideways, they move to a panel beside the photo so it gets the most room.

On the Website

Drag the photo with the mouse, and zoom with the mouse wheel, a trackpad pinch or the − and + buttons. The keyboard works too: the arrow keys move the photo (hold Shift to move it farther), + and − zoom, Enter uses the photo and Esc cancels.
