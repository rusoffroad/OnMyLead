Share Your Location

Choose who sees your location and for how long, from a few hours to until you turn it off. You can stop it any time.

iPhone AppLast Updated November 6, 2026

On This Page

  1. Share with Your Ride
  2. Share with Chosen Riders or Family
  3. Pick an End Date
  4. Location in the Background
  5. With No Service
  6. Who Sees What
  7. Stopping a Long Share

OnMyLead only shares your location when you turn it on, only with the people you choose, and only for as long as you pick. These rules are enforced by OnMyLead's server, not just the app.

There are two ways to share:

  • With a ride: everyone on the ride sees you on the Ride Mode map.

On a Freeride, if you're the leader, each rider's phone keeps the path you've ridden while they're in Ride Mode, built from the same live location they already see, to measure how far behind you everyone is. It stays on their phone, isn't uploaded, and is cleared after 18 hours or when they open another Freeride.

  • With chosen people: riders you pick, a family link, or both, from Share My Location.

Share with Your Ride

When you open Ride Mode on a live ride and aren't sharing yet, the app asks Share Your Location with This Ride?

  1. Open Ride Mode. See Use Ride Mode.
  2. Pick how long to share: 2 Hours, 4 Hours, 8 Hours or Until I Turn It Off.
  3. Or tap Pick an End Date, pick the day on the calendar (or type it), set Stop Sharing At, and tap the Share Until button. See Pick an End Date.
  4. If your iPhone asks for location access, allow it. Choose Always so the group keeps seeing you with the phone locked.

Only riders on this ride see you. Tap Not Now to skip. You can start sharing later by tapping the Off pill at the top of Ride Mode.

Check or Stop Ride Sharing

The pill at the top of Ride Mode shows the time left, such as 1h 20m, or On when you picked

Until I Turn It Off. A share that runs for more than a day shows days, such as 3d.

  1. Tap the pill. A banner shows Sharing and the time left.
  2. Tap Stop to stop sharing now, or Hide to fold the banner back into the pill.

When Ride Sharing Ends

Sharing with a ride stops on its own at the first of these:

  • Your timer runs out, or the end date you picked arrives.
  • The organizer ends or cancels the ride.
  • You leave the ride.
  • You tap Stop.

Even Until I Turn It Off stops when the ride ends or you leave it. Sharing with a ride never carries on after the ride.

Closing Ride Mode, or locking your phone, doesn't stop sharing. If the app was closed or your phone restarted, opening Ride Mode picks your share back up.

Share with Chosen Riders or Family

  1. Tap the You tab.
  2. Under On the Trail, tap Share My Location.
  3. Under Who Can See You, tap the riders you want. Only people you've been on a ride with are listed.
  4. To share with someone who doesn't have the app, turn on Family Link. See Share a Family Link.
  5. Under For How Long, pick 15 Min, 1 Hour, 4 Hours or 8 Hours, tap Until I Turn It Off, or tap Pick an End Date to choose a day and time on a calendar.

Until I Turn It Off has no time limit. It keeps sharing, even for days, until you tap Stop Sharing. The app asks you to confirm before it starts.

While you share, Share My Location shows a card for each share with Sharing Now and when it ends: the time left, the end date (such as until Sat, Nov 14, 6:00 PM), or until you turn it off. On the You tab, the Share My Location row also says you're sharing and until when, so a long share is never forgotten.

  • Tap Add 1 Hour to keep going longer. In the last 10 minutes, the button says Ending Soon · Add 1 Hr. A share set to Until I Turn It Off doesn't need it, so it isn't shown.
  • Tap Stop Sharing to end it now.

Pick an End Date

Both the ride sharing sheet and Share My Location let you pick exactly when sharing stops, up to a year out.

  1. Tap Pick an End Date. A calendar opens.
  2. Tap a day, or type the date in the box above the calendar. Use the arrows to change months.
  3. Check Stop Sharing At. It starts at 6:00 PM (or about an hour from now, later today). To change it, tap the time, then tap AM or PM, the hour and the minute.
  4. Tap the button, such as Share Until Sat, Nov 14, 6:00 PM.

A share with an end date still stops early if you tap Stop Sharing, and a ride share still stops when the ride ends.

Location in the Background

To keep your group updated while your phone is locked or in your pocket, OnMyLead needs location access set to Always. You can change it any time in your iPhone's Settings > OnMyLead > Location. While OnMyLead is using your location in the background, iPhone shows its location indicator.

On Android, allow location While Using the App. OnMyLead doesn't need Allow All the Time. While you share, Android shows a Sharing Your Location notification, and that is what keeps your group updated with the phone locked. Tap it to see who can see you or to stop sharing. On Android 13 and later, also allow notifications when asked, or that notification stays hidden.

If you turn off location for OnMyLead while you're sharing with a ride, Ride Mode shows a Location Is Off notice once, because your group can't see you move. Turn location back on for OnMyLead in your phone's Settings to keep sharing.

Keep Precise Location on for OnMyLead (on Android, Use precise location). With only an approximate location, your group sees you up to a few miles from where you are. See Make Your Location Accurate.

On Android, when you start sharing, OnMyLead asks once to change to a precise location if you allowed only an approximate one, and to turn on Google Location Accuracy if it's off.

Your phone sends a new position each time you move about 25 meters (80 feet), and at least every 15 seconds while you're stopped, so the group can tell you're still there.

To make your battery last longer, you can have your phone send less often with Battery Saver. It starts on Best Accuracy, the rate above.

With No Service

Live tracking needs a connection. When your phone has no service:

  • The group sees where you were last seen, with how long ago, never a dot that looks live.
  • While you share with a ride, your phone keeps recording your track. When you get service again, your current position goes up, and the points from the dead zone are added to your ride track for your ride summary.
  • Sharing still ends at its end time, even if your phone can't reach the server.

Offline maps keep the map on your screen without service, but positions only update when phones have a connection. See Offline Maps.

The exception is riders close by on the same ride: phones within Bluetooth range (about 30 to 100 meters) pass spots to each other with no service, encrypted for the ride only. Seeing those spots and sending yours over Bluetooth is part of OnMyLead Pro. See Share With Nearby Riders With No Service.

Who Sees What

  • Ride sharing: riders who have joined that ride.
  • Chosen riders: only the riders you picked are allowed to see your position.
  • Family link: anyone who has the link, until the share ends.

Stopping a Long Share

A share set to Until I Turn It Off or a far-off end date keeps your phone's location on in the background, which uses more battery. To stop it, tap the You tab, tap Share My Location, and tap Stop Sharing. On Android you can also tap the Sharing Your Location notification to get there. Battery Saver makes a long share easier on the battery.

Your exact location is never shown publicly. See Privacy.
