Release Notes

What changed in each version of OnMyLead for iPhone, iPad and Android, newest first. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play to get the latest.

  1. 1.0.1Android, Battery Saver and Trail Names

    Latest
    • iPhone and iPadComing to the App Store
    • AndroidGoogle Play, October 20, 2026

    OnMyLead arrives on Android phones and tablets. This update also adds Battery Saver, a ride calendar, Report a Bug, and trails that are easier to read.

    New

    • OnMyLead is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets, with the same rides, live map, chat and Pro plan as on iPhone and iPad. Android
    • Battery Saver on the You tab lets you choose how often your phone sends its location while you share: Best Accuracy, Balanced or Max Battery Saver.
    • Create a Ride has a Calendar button to pick any day from today up to a year out, plus Sat and Sun quick picks for the coming weekend.
    • Report a Bug on the You tab sends us your message with a screenshot, your phone model and app version, so problems get fixed faster.

    Improved

    • Trails are bolder and easier to see, with trail and road names on the map. Closed roads and trails, including today's seasonal closures, show in red with a Closed note.
    • Search for a park, trailhead, town or address on the waypoints map, the same way as in the route builder.

    Fixed

    • If you turn off location for OnMyLead while sharing, Ride Mode now tells you with a Location Is Off notice instead of saying you're still sharing.

  2. 1.0First Release

    • iPhone and iPadApp Store, October 28, 2026
    • AndroidIncluded in 1.0.1

    The first OnMyLead: plan a ride, bring your crew, and keep everyone together on one live map.

    New

    • Create a ride with a meeting point, time, route and who can join, then share it with a link, code or QR code.
    • Ride Mode puts the whole group on one live map, with status buttons, ride chat, polls and Ride Bubble alerts when someone falls behind.
    • Location sharing is per ride and temporary. Your exact location is never public, and you can stop sharing anytime.
    • Draw a route and add waypoints and planned stops. With Pro, import GPX files and save maps for offline use.
    • Find a ride near you and follow leaders you ride with. With Pro, keep your regular crew together in a riding group with its own chat.
    • Free lets you create one ride a month and join as many as you like, with every safety feature included. Pro adds unlimited rides, offline maps, GPX import and riding groups, with a 30-day free trial.
    • iPad gets its own layouts: the live map with a riders sidebar, and split views for rides and routes.
