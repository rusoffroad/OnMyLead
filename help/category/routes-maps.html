Routes and Maps

Draw or import a route, add stops, and save maps for offline use.

  • Draw a RouteTap the map to lay down your ride's route. It snaps to trails for you, and every rider sees it in Ride Mode.
  • Import a GPX RouteBring in a track from Gaia, onX or any GPX file and use it as your ride's route. A Pro feature.
  • Waypoints and Planned StopsMark camp, fuel, water or a hazard on a ride's map, and let the leader plan numbered stops for the whole group.
  • Check Your Fuel Range for a RideSee if your machine's tank covers the ride's miles, using the thirds rule, before you leave and in Ride Mode.
  • Map Layers and TrailsSwitch between satellite and topo, show public trails and roads with their names and closures, and tap a trail to see which vehicles can ride it.
  • Offline MapsDownload a ride's map or any area before you leave, so the map and route are still there when the signal drops. A Pro feature.
  • Get Directions in Apple Maps or Google MapsHand off to Apple Maps or Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions to the meeting point, along the route, to the destination, or to a rider.
  • Search for a PlaceFind a trailhead, off-road park, town, street address or coordinates for a meeting point, a destination or the map.
