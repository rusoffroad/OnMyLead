Riding Groups

Keep your regular crew together with a group and group chat.

  • Create and Manage a Riding GroupSave the people you ride with as a group, invite friends with a link, and invite the whole group to a ride in one tap.
  • Use Your Riding Group's ChatTalk with your whole riding group between rides, link a message to a ride, mute a busy group and clear messages.
  • Follow a Ride LeaderFollow the riders who organize and lead your favorite rides and get a notification when they post a new public ride.
