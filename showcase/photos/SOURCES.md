All five are U.S. Bureau of Land Management (BLM California) photos published on Flickr with the
Public Domain Mark, so they can be used commercially with no attribution required (the site's footer
credits BLM anyway). They are cropped, resized and saved as WebP here. Swap in RUS Offroad photos any
time by replacing these files with the same names.
